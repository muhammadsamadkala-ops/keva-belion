Frequently asked questions

Everything about ordering, delivery, payment and returns. Can't find your answer — message us on WhatsApp.

Is Cash on Delivery available everywhere in Pakistan?

Yes. Cash on Delivery is available nationwide. We also accept Bank Transfer, and a card payment option at checkout.

How long does delivery take?

Orders within Karachi usually arrive in 1–2 working days. For other cities across Pakistan, delivery typically takes 3–5 working days depending on your location.

Are these perfumes made by Kiva Belion, or are you reselling?

We formulate, mix and bottle every fragrance ourselves in Karachi. Nothing is bought in bulk from another brand and relabeled — this is our own work, from a single idea to the finished bottle.

Can I return or exchange a perfume if I don't like the scent?

Because of hygiene reasons, we can only accept returns on unopened, unused bottles within 7 days of delivery. See our Returns & Refunds page for the full policy and how to start a return.

What if my order arrives damaged or wrong?

Message us on WhatsApp with your order number and a photo within 48 hours of delivery, and we'll sort out a replacement or refund at no extra cost to you.

How long do your perfumes last on skin?

Most of our fragrances last 8–9 hours on skin, longer on clothing and fabric. Our Scent Journey section explains how a fragrance develops from the first spray through to the base notes.

Do you ship outside Pakistan?

Not yet — we currently deliver within Pakistan only. We're working on international shipping and will announce it on our socials when it's ready.

Can I pay online instead of Cash on Delivery?

Yes, Bank Transfer is available at checkout, and we're rolling out card payments. If you'd rather pay online right away, message us on WhatsApp and we'll help you sort it out directly.

Do you offer sample sizes or testers?

Not at the moment, but it's something we're considering as the collection grows. Follow our socials for updates.

How do I know which fragrance is right for me?

Try our Scent Quiz — a few quick questions and we'll point you to the fragrance that fits you best.
