Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and who sees it — in plain language.

Who we are

Kiva Belion is a perfume brand based in Karachi, Pakistan. This page explains what information we collect when you use this website or order from us, and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you place an order, you give us your name, phone number, delivery address and city, the items you want, and an optional gift message. You send this to us yourself in a WhatsApp message. We also keep any messages you send us by WhatsApp, email or social media (for example, a question or a return request).

This website does not take card payments, and we do not ask for or store card details.

If you subscribe to our updates, we collect the email address you enter.

How we use it

We use your order details only to confirm and deliver your order, contact you about it, handle returns and refunds, and reply to your questions. If you subscribe, we use your email address only to send you Kiva Belion news such as new fragrances and restocks, and you can unsubscribe at any time by messaging us. We do not sell your information.

Who else sees it

  • Delivery partners. Our courier receives your name, phone number and address so they can deliver your order.
  • WhatsApp. Orders are sent through WhatsApp, which is run by Meta and handles messages under its own privacy policy.
  • Email sign-up. When you subscribe, your email address is passed to us through FormSubmit, a form-processing service, which delivers it to our inbox and sends you the welcome email.
  • Google Fonts. This site loads its fonts from Google, so Google may receive your IP address when a page loads.
  • Social links. Our Instagram, Facebook and TikTok links take you to those sites, which have their own policies.

Cookies and your cart

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not run analytics at the moment. Your shopping bag is kept only in your browser while the page is open; it is not stored on our servers.

How long we keep it

We keep order details for as long as we need them to deliver your order, deal with any return or complaint, and keep basic business records.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the personal details we hold about you by messaging us on WhatsApp or emailing kevibelion@gmail.com. We will do this unless we need to keep something for a legitimate reason, such as an unresolved order.

Changes to this page

If we change how we handle information (for example, if we add analytics or online payments), we will update this page. The date below shows when it was last changed.
