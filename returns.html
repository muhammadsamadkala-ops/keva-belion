Returns & refunds

We want you to be happy with your order. Here's exactly how returns and refunds work.

7-day return window

You can return a fragrance within 7 days of delivery, as long as the bottle is unopened, unused and in its original packaging. Because perfume is a hygiene product, we're unable to accept returns on bottles that have been opened or used, unless the product arrived damaged or faulty.

Damaged or wrong item

If your order arrives damaged, leaking, or isn't what you ordered, message us on WhatsApp with your order number and a photo within 48 hours of delivery. We'll arrange a free replacement or a full refund — whichever you prefer.

How to start a return

  1. Message us on WhatsApp or email with your order number and the reason for the return.
  2. We'll confirm whether it's eligible and share pickup or drop-off details.
  3. Once we receive the item and check it's unopened and unused, we'll process your refund.

Refund method

Refunds are sent via bank transfer to your account, usually within 5–7 working days of us receiving the returned item. If you paid Cash on Delivery, we'll ask for your bank details to process the transfer.

Exchanges

Want a different scent instead? Let us know when you start your return — we're happy to exchange an unopened bottle for a different fragrance of equal value, or adjust the difference if the price varies.

Still need help?

Reach out any time on WhatsApp or email — we usually reply within a few hours.
