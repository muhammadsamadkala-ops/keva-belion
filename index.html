How it unfolds

A fragrance isn't one smell — it's three, released in sequence. This is how every Kiva Belion scent moves across a day.

0–15 minutes

Top notes

The first impression — citrus peel, bergamot, cardamom. Bright and brief, it introduces the scent and fades as it warms on skin.

15 minutes – 4 hours

Heart notes

The character of the fragrance — rose, saffron, jasmine. This is what people notice when they lean in.

4 hours onward

Base notes

What lingers — oud, amber, musk. The notes that stay once the rest has faded.
