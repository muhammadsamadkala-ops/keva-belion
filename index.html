Built on restraint

Most perfume houses chase more — more notes, more noise, more shelf space. We do the opposite. Every Kiva Belion scent starts as a single idea, held until it's worth wearing.

We don't resell — we formulate, blend and bottle every fragrance ourselves in small batches, with ingredients we can trace back to source. A scent doesn't join the collection until it earns its place next to the other five. That's why we stay at six.

How it unfolds

A fragrance isn't one smell — it's three, released in sequence. This is how every Kiva Belion scent moves across a day.

0–15 minutes

Top notes

The first impression — citrus peel, bergamot, cardamom. Bright and brief, it introduces the scent and fades as it warms on skin.

15 minutes – 4 hours

Heart notes

The character of the fragrance — rose, saffron, jasmine. This is what people notice when they lean in.

4 hours onward

Base notes

What lingers — oud, amber, musk. The reason a scent is still on your scarf the next morning.
